Craft

How I work

  1. I

    Real data, full scale.

    No synthetic demos, no mock results. If it can't run on production data, it doesn't ship.

  2. II

    Held-out evaluation.

    Challengers earn promotion with measured wins on unseen data — never on vibes.

  3. III

    QA at every step.

    Pipelines with gates: a failure stops the line, it doesn't get papered over.

  4. IV

    Products, not decks.

    The proof is something people can touch — a game, a map, a tool.
