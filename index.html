Craft

How I work.

  • Real data, full scale. No synthetic demos, no mock results. If it can't run on production data, it doesn't ship.
  • Held-out evaluation. Challengers earn promotion with measured wins on unseen data — never on vibes.
  • QA at every step. Pipelines with gates: a failure stops the line, it doesn't get papered over.
  • Products, not decks. The proof is something people can touch — a game, a map, a tool.
